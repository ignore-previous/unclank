## D09-N02 Standard option formatting and avoided true/false misuse

- Not a tell. consistent, homogeneous options of about equal length are good item-writing practice, and AI items rarely misuse the true/false format (7.6% vs 25.4% in teacher-only items, Frontiers, Mixtral-8x7B). ## D09-N03 Cue flaws as proof of AI authorship

The same Frontiers study found teacher-AI items worse than either source alone, so teacher review does not remove them; and Hong Kong human items had more implausible distractors (7) than AI items (3).
