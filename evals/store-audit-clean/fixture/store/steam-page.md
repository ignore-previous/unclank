# Steam store page: Saltmarsh Signal

## Short description

Repair old shortwave radios in a drowned delta town and hear what the
radios have been carrying.

## About this game

Nine quests, nine people, one town that is slowly going under water. You mend
radios, tune them, and decide what to do with what you hear.

- Backgrounds and character art by Ines Okonkwo-Bell (see the AI disclosure)
- Marsh channels generated from a seed, around a hand-built town
- Nine quests with clear steps and one-time rewards

## AI disclosure

The radio announcer is an AI-generated voice. We chose it because the voice
is meant to sound synthetic. Three background plates began as image-model
drafts that Ines Okonkwo-Bell painted over, and some ambient lines began as
language-model drafts that Tomasz Wierzbicki rewrote. Our code editor has an
AI assistant, which players do not see. The full list is in our asset
register.

## Capsule and key art

Main capsule and key art are hand-painted by Ines Okonkwo-Bell. No image model
was used for either.
