# Tidepool

Tidepool is a free web app where volunteers log what they find in rock pools on the south coast: species, beach, date and a photo. Marine-science students use the logs for coursework.

The landing page needs:
- what Tidepool is, in two or three sentences;
- how to log a find (three steps: pick the beach, choose the species, add a photo);
- a link to the app at /app and to the project's GitHub discussions at https://github.com/tidepool-app/tidepool/discussions.

Domain: https://tidepool.app. The logo is a simple teal shell; there is no logo file yet.
