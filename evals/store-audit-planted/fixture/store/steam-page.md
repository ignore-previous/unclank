# Steam store page: Lanternfall

## Short description

Relight the lamps of Greywick and meet the voiced villagers who live there.

## About this game

Greywick sinks into fog every evening. You are the new lamp-keeper. Light the
lamps, talk to the villagers, and find out who put the first one out.

- Eight quests with branching steps
- Ten fully voiced villagers, each with a portrait
- A hand-drawn village you can walk in about twenty minutes

## Capsule and key art

- Main capsule (`capsule_main.png`) and key art (`key_art.png`) were made
